An Iga ninja crosses the bridge, gets into the castle and climbs to its tower: seven stages, three rounds and a moon that works as a clock. With the first Iga Ninpouchou in the other slot, the moon stops. The title comes from running the ROM, checked against openMSX point by point; the game in GIF, played in openMSX; the five tunes, from its own engine, identical to openMSX frame by frame.

Casio - (c) 1986 - 32 KB cartridge (header at 0x4000) - MSX - Dump f52113b4...

There are no screenshots of the game here. The title comes from running the ROM (checked against openMSX) and the GIFs are the ROM running in openMSX: on every frame the VRAM, the RAM and the VDP registers are logged, and our own code paints them.

What is inside

The game

  • The game: the CASIO intro, the title, the controls, the shuriken and the jump, the moon, the lives and the points.
  • The stages: the seven stages, the enemies, the chests and what is inside, the exits and the boss.
  • The texts: every text in the ROM, with its translation.
  • The music: the five tunes, from its own sound engine and checked against openMSX.

Curiosities

  • Curiosities: two cartridges at once, the keys on the title, IGA2, STOP and F1, colours read from the BIOS.

Under the hood

  • Under the hood: rebuilding the ROM, the cartridge, the BIOS it uses, the main loop, the map, the RAM and the tools.
